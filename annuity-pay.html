How resetting works

1 · Close the life sale

Every closed life client is a potential annuity lead.

2 · Reset to the annuity team

Set the client with the annuity team for a retirement/annuity conversation.

3 · Get paid at your life level

When the annuity places, you earn the commission % that matches your life level.
