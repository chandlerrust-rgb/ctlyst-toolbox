Lead Sources Guide

Where your conversations come from decides your cost per sale. Start with one or two sources, track the numbers in the Lead Spend Planner, and scale what works.

Lead types

HIGHEST INTENT

Inbound calls

The prospect calls you, live. Highest close rate and highest price per lead. Best for full-time agents who can be on the phone during business hours.

HIGH INTENT

Google search leads

People who searched for coverage and filled out a form. Strong intent, costs more per lead, close rate well above social leads.

VOLUME

Facebook / Meta leads

Cheap and plentiful, but lower intent. Speed matters most here: call within 5 minutes or contact rates drop fast.

FILTERED

Text-verified leads

Social leads that confirmed their number by text. Fewer junk numbers, but many still won't pick up. Work them with text first.

LOW COST

Aged leads

Leads that are weeks or months old, sold for a fraction of the price. Low contact rate, but great for practice and cheap volume.

SLOW, STEADY

Direct mail

Mailers that people return. Slower to arrive, but older prospects tend to trust it, and it works well for final expense.

FREE

Referrals

The best leads you'll get. Ask every client at the end of every sale, and again on the welcome call.

Before you buy from any vendor

  • Ask if the leads are exclusive or shared, and how many agents get each one.
  • Ask about the return / credit policy for bad numbers.
  • Make sure the leads are TCPA-compliant with documented consent.
  • Start with a small test order and track contact rate, close rate and cost per sale.
  • Ask your upline which vendors the team is getting results from right now.
