Pitch Flowchart

Every call follows the same path. Know where you are on the map and you'll never get lost, even if the words come out different every time.

The phone call, start to finish

1. Intro & reason for the call

Say who you are and why you're calling: they asked for information about coverage. Smile, slow down, and use their name.

↳ Doesn't remember filling it out → remind them what they asked for and when
↳ Bad time → book a specific callback time, don't just say "later"

2. Set the agenda

Tell them how the call will go: a few questions, look at options together, and they decide. People relax when they know the plan.

3. Fact-find: the why

Why did they reach out? Who would be left with the bills? What would happen if they passed tomorrow? Let them tell the story. This is the most important step.

↳ Short answers → ask "what made you look into this now?" and wait

4. Health & lifestyle questions

Age, height and weight, tobacco, medications and conditions. This tells you which carriers and products they qualify for.

↳ Serious health history → guaranteed or graded options, and set expectations honestly

5. Budget & coverage amount

Agree on what the coverage needs to do (final expenses, the mortgage, income replacement) and a monthly amount that's comfortable, not a stretch.

6. Present options

Show 2–3 options, good, better and best, from the carriers they qualify for. Recommend one and explain why in plain words.

↳ Objection → go to Objections & Word Tracks, then come back here

7. Close & application

Ask for the decision directly. Then fill out the e-app together: beneficiary, health questions, payment date that lines up with their income.

8. Lock it in

Explain what happens next (underwriting, the welcome call, the policy in the mail). Ask for referrals while the good feeling is fresh.

Rules of the road

  • Never skip the fact-find. Without a why, every price sounds too high.
  • Ask, then stop talking. Silence is where the real answers come from.
  • Only recommend what you'd put your own family in.
  • Tell the truth about health questions. Misstated apps get denied at claim time, and that's the worst outcome for the family.
