How You’re Paid

The different ways you can earn, from your first sale to running a team. Each one links to the full details.

1

Commission on your own sales

Every agent

You earn a commission level on the life premium you write. Everyone starts at 80% and moves up as their production grows. Hit a level's numbers two months in a row to be promoted. No skipping levels.

80% (new agent) up to 145%EverLife Comp Plan →
2

Moving up by building a team

Agents who recruit

Levels 105%–110% can be reached with personal or team issued premium; 115% and up are based on team issued premium plus direct agents each issuing $25K+ a month.

115% at $250K team up to 145% at $2M teamEverLife Comp Plan →
3

Monthly production bonus

Every producing agent

A cash bonus each month based on your issued, paid life volume. FFL's Producer Bonus also adds a lead voucher (1% of your bonus level, for FFL Exclusive Leads) and a Writer Increase Bonus when you add writers and team volume.

$650 at $20K up to $20,000 at $200K a monthFFL Producer Bonus (+ estimator) →
4

VP bonus pool

Vice Presidents

FFL sets aside 4.5% of monthly life premium. 75% is split between VPs by points (bonusable premium, direct legs, writers, non-VP teams, persistency); 25% goes to VPs who promoted directs to 145.

Share of the monthly poolFFL VP Bonus Program (+ video) →
5

Annuities

Life agents (resetting) and annuity producers

Reset your closed life clients to the annuity team and earn a rate based on your life level, or write annuities yourself and earn by year-to-date annuity volume.

3.25% up to 6.50%EverLife Annuity Pay Scale →
6

Medicare (SEP)

Agents writing Medicare during the SEP

A $125 base commission per approved/effectuated policy plus a placement-rate bonus per active policy. Pick the Performance Plan (bigger bonuses, standard chargebacks) or the Stability Plan (smaller bonuses, no routine chargebacks); your choice is locked for the SEP. Managers, team leads and recruiters earn per-policy overrides.

$125 up to $300 per policyEverLife Medicare Commissions →
