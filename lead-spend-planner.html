How the budget is split
  • Under $30 an hour. Every dollar goes to aged leads at $1 each, up to the leads you can dial per hour.
  • $30 to $64. One Inbound Mortgage Protection lead per hour. Aged leads fill the gap.
  • $65 to $84. One Inbound Mortgage Protection and one Inbound Final Expense lead per hour. Aged leads fill the gap.
  • $85 and up. One Inbound Mortgage Protection, one Inbound Final Expense and one Meta Ad Pool lead per hour. Aged leads fill the gap.

Spend per hour is the weekly budget divided by weekly hours. Each paid channel switches on at one per hour once the hour can pay for it and everything before it, so the cutoffs follow the lead costs in Assumptions. Daily numbers are the weekly numbers divided by days worked. Closes are leads times the close rate. AP is closes times AP per deal. Commission is AP times the commission rate. The advance is the share of that commission paid up front; the rest is paid as it is earned.
