Get Licensed Weeks 1–2

Study and pass the licensing exam for your home state (budget 2 weeks), then get contracted on our eight-carrier shelf and set up E&O coverage.

Train & Run Wingmans Weeks 1–6

Start EverLife's 30 Days to Pro the same week you begin licensing. Shadow closers, then run your own appointments as the wingman before going solo.

Apply for a stack of 0% intro-APR business cards for $15K in free starting capital. It funds ad spend and training costs with none of it out of pocket.

Production & Team
4

Start Selling Ongoing

Get in front of prospects consistently and write $20K–$100K in AP per month as your standard, sustainable pace. It's the proof the system works before you teach it.

5

Build Your Life Team Months 3–12

Show your results, market yourself, and share the opportunity. Recruit selectively into the same path, then override their production as the team grows.

6

Add Annuities Months 6–12

Life agents reset closed clients as potential annuity leads to the annuity team. Every life sale becomes a second opportunity. You earn your life level's annuity rate (3.25%–6.50%); see the annuity pay scale.

Leverage & Wealth Conversion
7

Build the Medicare Branch Once self-sustaining

Set your monthly lifestyle number. Every dollar of life-team profit above it rolls into a Medicare hiring fund: a second, recurring-revenue branch that compounds through renewals.

8

Set Aside 20% for Taxes, Roll Into RV Parks Every year

Reserve 20% of net profit for taxes, then deploy it alongside Chandler in RV park & MH acquisitions, where bonus depreciation and cost segregation shelter the very liability it was set aside for.
