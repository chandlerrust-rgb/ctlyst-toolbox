Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Steelwater Strategies, operated by Chandler Rust, an independent licensed insurance agent ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses and protects information you provide on scalewithsteelwater.com and through our phone calls and text messages.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address and anything else you choose to share when you fill out a form, call, text or email us.
  • Basic technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site working.

How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about the information you requested, including the independent insurance agent opportunity and, where you've asked, insurance products.
  • To schedule and confirm appointments and send reminders.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our texting platform), and only as needed to provide the service.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for full messaging terms.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With service providers that help us operate (for example, website hosting, CRM, scheduling and messaging tools), who may use it only to provide services to us;
  • With insurance carriers, only if you choose to apply for coverage; and
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP, or ask us not to call you.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law. We will honor those requests as required.

Children

Our site and services are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
