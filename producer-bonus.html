Lead voucher & writer increase

Lead voucher

1% of your production bonus level, for FFL Exclusive Leads only. Hit the $50K level → $500 voucher; $100K → $1,000.

Writer increase bonus

Compared to your best month in the last 12. You need both numbers. Personal producer only, not their upline.

Estimate your bonus

Any producer receiving a bonus must be in good standing with FFL. FFL reserves the right to garnish any lead debt, carrier debt, licensing fees or any outstanding balance that has rolled to FFL from the personal production bonus. Volume is based on the calendar month. The minimum amount a producer can earn is $650.
