What other states should I license in?

The more states you're licensed in, the more leads you'll have, the lower your cost per lead, and the wider your funnel. Each state has a cost to get reciprocity, and some are more worth it than others. Use this grouping guide to decide which ones are right for you. Work top to bottom.

1

Your home state

Start here

Get licensed where you live first. Every other state is added through reciprocity off this license.
