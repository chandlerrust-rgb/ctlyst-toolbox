Bonusable Premium max 30 pts

Your Qualified BP as a share of corporate BP, compared to the top producer's share, × 30.

Direct Legs max 5 pts

Number of direct producing legs ÷ 6 (capped at 5).

Net Direct Writers max 5 pts

Current direct writers minus your best month in the last 12, per the points table.

# of Direct Non-VP max 5 pts

Non-VP direct teams with at least $10,000 team volume, per the points table. Need 2 for the first point.

Persistency max 5 pts

Average of short-term (6-month) and long-term (12–14 month) persistency, per the points table.

Estimate your VP bonus

Any VP receiving a bonus must be in good standing with FFL. FFL reserves the right to subtract any lead debt, carrier debt, or any outstanding balance that was rolled to FFL from the VP bonus. If you have contracts outside of FFL, you are not eligible for a bonus. Effective 03/01/26.
